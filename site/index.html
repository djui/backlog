BackLog

Watch later, read later — offline. One queue for the videos, reels, articles and links you mean to get to, and a history of everything you did.

Download for Mac Source on GitHub

Free. macOS 26 or later on Apple silicon. Signed and notarized; updates itself. iPhone app in TestFlight — your list syncs through iCloud.

Save from anywhere

Share → BackLog from Safari, Messages or WhatsApp, press ⌥⌘L in any browser, paste or drop a link. A whole chat message works too — the text becomes a note, with who sent it.

Offline, small by default

Videos download at 480p (about 4 MB a minute) with captions. Articles are saved in a clean reader with their pictures. Reels keep their caption and picture even when they can't be downloaded.

Summaries you can scale

A one-line takeaway, the key points, or detailed notes — made on your Mac by Apple's on-device model. Timestamps jump right to the moment.

Skim mode

Go through your queue as summaries: watch it, keep just the notes, or skip it. J/K to move, W/N/X to decide.

Find it in two years

Search by words in titles, captions, transcripts and article text, by what's in the preview picture, or just by what you remember it was about.

Tidy by itself

Keeps the next few videos ready and deletes what you've watched after a few days. The item, its summary and transcript stay in History forever.

History you own

Exports everything you've watched and read as Markdown — one file a day — plus JSON lines, into any folder, for your journal or life log.

Private

No account, no server. Downloads, summaries, picture recognition and search all run on your Mac.

Install

  1. Download the DMG from the latest release and drag BackLog to Applications.
  2. Open it. It fetches the video downloader (yt-dlp) on first use and keeps it current.
  3. For summaries, turn on Apple Intelligence in System Settings.
BackLog downloads what you ask it to for your own offline viewing; respect the sites' terms and creators' rights. Made by djui.
